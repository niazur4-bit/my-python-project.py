About the firm

Built on ledgers, not shortcuts

Munib and Co was founded to give individuals and small businesses the same standard of accounting rigor that large corporates expect from their in-house teams.

Our story

Two decades of getting the numbers right

What started as a single practitioner's office handling tax returns for local traders in Swat has grown into a full-service accounting practice. We still take the same approach we started with: read every document, verify every figure, and never file anything we haven't checked ourselves.

Today we support sole proprietors, partnerships and private limited companies with everything from monthly bookkeeping to statutory audits and SECP incorporation.
